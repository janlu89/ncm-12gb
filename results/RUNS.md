# Run-by-run report

Generated by `results/make_report.py` from the files in `results/runs/`. Do not edit by hand.

117 runs: 20 tokenizers, 92 generators with a Gen. PPL measurement, 5 generators interrupted or cancelled before evaluation.

How to read it:

- **Gen. PPL**: generative perplexity of 1,280 generated stories (256 for each of 5 sampling seeds) under GPT-2 large; lower is better. **± eval** is the standard deviation across the 5 sampling seeds of the *same* model, not across training seeds.
- **Entropy**: entropy (nats) of the token frequencies inside each generated story, averaged over the stories; a drop signals repetitive text.
- **Val loss**: for generators, mean cross-entropy (nats) over the predicted codes on 4,096 validation stories; for tokenizers, their own validation loss. Neither is comparable between different tokenizers.
- **Corruption** is the fraction of input codes replaced by random ones during training, per level: `constant 0.5` on every level, or `linear 0.75 to 0.25` from the coarsest to the finest level (not over time). **Flex** = upstream FlexAttention switched on. **LR** is the peak learning rate.
- **Init from data** = codebooks initialised with k-means; **dead-code restart** = unused codes re-seeded from the batch, only up to the given step; **level-0 norm** = normalisation of the level-0 path.
- **Stories seen** = steps × batch. **Seed** is the training seed (model initialisation, corruption noise); the order of the training data is the same in every run.
- **Min** is wall-clock time from the first to the last logged event, including validation, compilation and checkpoints, excluding time spent paused. A run resumed after an interruption counts the repeated steps twice. **VRAM** is the peak memory allocated by PyTorch, in GiB.
- In `runs_index.csv`, `steps` and `stories_seen` are the planned values; `last_step` is the last step logged.
- Run names are explained in `results/README.md`.
- Each run's exact settings are in `results/runs/<run>/config.yaml`.
